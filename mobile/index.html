Output safety

Outputs stay disabled until you unlock controls and enable them here. The firmware also drops every output to neutral after 500 ms without valid RC frames.

Anyone connected to this WiFi network can send commands once unlocked. Use a WPA2 password on the ESP32 access point and keep outputs disabled on the bench.

Link statistics before the last drop — a slide is range, a cliff is interference

RX_SILENT — no CRSF bytes at all: the receiver stopped transmitting (power, ground or its TX wire). RF_LINK_LOST — the receiver is still talking but reports 0% link quality. RC_LOST — channel frames stopped while the receiver kept transmitting. RX_GAP — the CRSF stream paused for 150 ms–1 s but the receiver kept its state: the wire opened, it did not reboot. RX_RESTART — the stream stopped and the receiver came back cold (0 dBm on both antennas): it lost power and booted, even if the drop was too fast to see on the rail. WIRE_NOISE — damaged frames on the receiver UART (bad CRC or lost sync): the cable, plug or ground, not the radio. PWR_DIP — the 5V rail dropped below the threshold (depth and duration recorded); needs the optional ADC tap. PWR_GLITCH — hardware-latched supply assertion, caught even mid-rub; needs the optional power-good input. BOOT — the ESP32 restarted (its reason is recorded: brownout, watchdog, power-on…).
